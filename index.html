Sunday roast planner

Dinner for two: herbed, basted beef silverside pulled rare, Maris Piper roasties, roast carrots, cavolo nero, granule gravy with beef pot and wine, and Jamie's yorkies. The oven stays at one temperature from start to finish, and every opening of the door is planned. Tick things off, hit the timer buttons, and the bar above always shows what is next.

The beef

46°C pull from oven
Rare, as you asked: pull at 46°C in the thickest part. Under a tight foil and tea towel tent it climbs about 4 to 6°C, finishing around 50 to 52°C. Silverside is a lean, firm cut, so slice it thin across the grain. Keep the string on while it roasts and snip it off before carving.
Pull at 44°C: very rare, finishes about 49°C Pull at 46°C: rare, finishes about 51°C Pull at 48°C: your maximum, finishes about 53°C
Rare is only safe for a whole joint where just the outside has touched anything. If the pack says rolled, or the joint is several pieces tied together, the FSA says it should not be served rare. Check the label and the cut ends before you commit. At 200°C expect a thicker grey band under the crust than a slow roast would give.

The whole afternoon at a glance

Faded bars are waiting time. The red dots are every moment the oven door opens. The beef roast bar is an estimate (about 50 to 65 minutes at 200°C to reach 46°C): the probe decides.

Oven door moments

Eight openings, each one batching every job into a single go. Between them the door stays shut, so the oven stays at one temperature. After 17:16 it does not open again until everything comes out.

What sits on which oven shelf

One oven, one temperature, never more than three things in at once. Set the shelves at step 2 so the beef tin clears the shelf above it.

      If it goes sideways

      Beef hits 46°C early

      Take it out at the next door moment, or straight away if it is clearly there, and tent it tightly. It holds heat for about 40 minutes. Everything else carries on exactly as planned.

      Beef is not at 44°C by 17:00

      Leave it in and keep the oven where it is. Pick a later serve time above and every step after 15:00 slides back to match. Do not skip the rest.

      Yorkies are the fussy bit

      They need bubbling oil and a closed door. If the oil is not sizzling when the batter goes in, put the tin back for 2 minutes first. The beef is out of the oven by then, so nothing is in the way.

      Potatoes darkening too fast

      Shuffle the tray down a shelf at the next door moment. They can sit in the switched-off oven for 10 minutes without harm.
